First public release.

- Hold a key, speak and release: your speech is transcribed on your Mac and tidied up by a local language model, then pasted where you were typing. Your speech never leaves your Mac.
- Question mode, editing selected text by voice, and transcription of audio and video files.
- Interface and dictation in English, Português (Brasil), Español, Français and 简体中文 (Spanish dictation is marked beta).
- Automatic updates (a daily check of the GitHub releases; it can be turned off).
